BLAUPUNKT – MAKING ITS POINT, LOUD AND CLEAR.
Blaupunkt's meteoric rise started with a Blue Point. Back in
1923, when Germany woke up to the first sounds of a radio
broadcast, it coincided with the birth of the Ideal Company in
Berlin. The fledgling enterprise began its journey with the
manufacture of Headphones, which were arduously tested by
skilled technicians and labelled with a symbol of quality, the
Blue Point.
Soon, customers only had to ask for the Blue Point
Headphones and owing to its rising popularity, the symbol of
quality became a trusted trademark. So much so, that the
company even adopted it as its very own brand name in 1938.
Today after so many decades, when one talks about the best
and the most innovative name in Car Infotainment, Blaupunkt
easily enjoys a sound edge over the rest.
